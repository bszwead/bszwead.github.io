Your neighborhood place to gather.

Great coffee is only part of what makes a coffee shop special. Neighborfolk Coffee Co. is a welcoming place to slow down, connect with others, and feel at home.

Welcome to Neighborfolk

Neighborfolk Coffee Co. was created around the idea of a third place: somewhere outside of home and work where people can gather, relax, study, work, or catch up with friends. We want to serve quality coffee in a space that feels comfortable, familiar, and welcoming.

More Than Coffee

Quality Drinks

From classic coffee favorites to matcha and seasonal specialty drinks, our menu will have something for every kind of coffee break.

A place to Belong

Whether you're meeting a friend, getting some work done, or just enjoying a quiet moment, Neighborfolk is a place where you're welcome to stay awhile.

Community

We believe a great neighborhood coffee shop can bring people together and become a familiar part of the community it serves.
